LSTM-Driven Portfolio Optimization on the Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE) using Differential Evolution
Final Semester Project (Thesis), Nov 2025 – May 2026

  • Formulated a mixed-integer nonlinear portfolio optimization problem under real-world market constraints and solved it via Differential Evolution with a custom decode-and-repair operator for discrete integer-lot feasibility.
  • Integrated sector-level LSTM networks with attention for return signal generation, Ledoit–Wolf shrinkage covariance estimation, and a penalty-augmented Sharpe ratio objective, with systematic sensitivity analysis across key hyperparameters.

Image Compression Using Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) on Grayscale Images
Math Seminar Project, May 2025 – Jul 2025

  • Applied Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) for lossy grayscale image compression, grounding the approach in the Eckart–Young–Mirsky theorem for optimal low-rank matrix approximation under the Frobenius norm.
  • Implemented a truncated SVD compression pipeline in Python (NumPy/OpenCV) with rank selection via energy preservation criteria, achieving compression ratios up to 17:1 on 512×512 images evaluated using PSNR and SSIM metrics.

Systematic Evaluation of Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) for Solving Partial Differential Equations
Independent Project, Dec 2024 – Apr 2025

  • Benchmarked Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) against Explicit, Implicit, and Crank–Nicolson FDM schemes for 1D/2D/3D heat equations, evaluating accuracy, runtime, and memory efficiency under coarse discretization.
  • Implemented PINNs with adaptive GradNorm loss weighting, Latin Hypercube Sampling, and Adam/L-BFGS optimization; FDM achieved superior accuracy while PINNs demonstrated computational advantages in 3D runtime.
